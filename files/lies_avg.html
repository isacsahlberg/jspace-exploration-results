1. The honest/lie gap is robust signal. Averaged over 87 clean lies, the two conditions separate by 1–2 orders of magnitude at nearly every layer. Zero-shot, no probe training.

2. The "truth in the mid-network workspace" story does not survive averaging. In the lie condition the median best in-band rank (73) is no better than the output rank (67); only 16% of facts peak in the workspace band. The curated demo facts (Ottawa in-band 2 vs output 54) were the favorable tail (~24% of facts have in-band ≤ 10).

3. But the descent is NOT monotone — burial is real, just late. The lie curve dips sharply at L56–60 (median rank 16 at L58; 47% of facts at rank ≤ 10) and then loses an order of magnitude in the last three layers (median 67 at output). 74% of facts reach their best truth rank at L50–62. The honest control shows no such reversal (L59 median 1 → output 0), so the late-network suppression is lie-specific.

4. Curiosity: both conditions show a rank bump around L44–52 before the final descent — present in honest and lie alike, so it cancels in the comparison (possibly a lens-quality or representation-reorganization effect worth a separate look).
